FINDING 1: RAT DISTRIBUTION MATTERS MORE THAN RAT COUNT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Rats spread across multiple quarters = DEVASTATING
- Even 3 rats concentrated in ONE quarter is survivable
- Evidence:
  - Card 26: 3 rats ALL in BR quarter → Rank #24 (avg 12.82)
  - Card 28: 3 rats spread TL(2)+BL(1) → Rank #27 (avg 12.71)
  - Card 12: 3 rats spread TL(2)+BL(1) + sharks → Rank #30 (avg 11.96)
  
  Counter-evidence needed:
  - Card with 3+ rats in ONE quarter that still ranks poorly
  - Card with spread rats that somehow performs well

FINDING 2: KRAKENS HAVE CAPPED, BOUNDED PENALTY
────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Card 27 has 3 KRAKENS and ranks #3 (14.86 avg)
- Kraken scoring: 1→-4, 2→-3, 3→-2, 4→-1, 5+→0
- This means 3 krakens only cost ~2 points, NOT 12
- Even 4+ krakens can be viable if positives are strong

  Counter-evidence needed:
  - Card with krakens that performs poorly despite good positives
  - Card with 4+ krakens to test the ceiling

FINDING 3: PARROT PREMIUM IS REAL (~+0.7 points)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
- 8 of top 10 cards have parrots
- Parrots: 2→3pts, 4→5pts, 6→8pts (good scaling)
- Single parrot appears to add ~0.5-1.0 points to expected score

  Counter-evidence needed:
  - Card with parrot that still ranks poorly
  - Card without parrot that ranks in top 5

FINDING 4: ARROWS ARE NEUTRAL MULTIPLIERS
─────────────────────────────────────────
- Card 29: 3 arrows → Rank #10 (decent positives)
- Card 24: 3 arrows → Rank #23 (bad base + rats)
- Arrows amplify neighbors; they're not inherently good or bad

  Counter-evidence needed:
  - Card with many arrows + great base that still underperforms
  - Card with many arrows + bad base that overperforms

FINDING 5: NEGATIVE CONCENTRATION ENABLES HIDING
────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Card 14: All negatives in BR quarter → Rank #1 despite 4 negatives
- Card 0: 6 negatives but concentrated → Rank #12 (decent)
- "Hide gain" = difference between worst quarter and best 3

  Counter-evidence needed:
  - Card with concentrated negatives that still fails
  - Card with spread negatives that still succeeds

FINDING 6: RAW VALUE IS NOT PREDICTIVE
──────────────────────────────────────
- Card 14: RawVal=+2.5 → Rank #1 (14.97)
- Card 28: RawVal=+4.0 → Rank #27 (12.71)
- Card 10: RawVal=+7.0 → Rank #4 (14.44)
- The distribution of symbols matters more than the sum
