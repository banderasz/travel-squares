HYPOTHESIS TESTING RESULTS
================================================================================

FINDING 1: RAT DISTRIBUTION > RAT COUNT
Status: ✅ STRONGLY CONFIRMED

Evidence:
  - Card 107 (3 rats in 3 quarters): DEAD LAST at 11.82
  - Card 34 (2 rats in 2 quarters): #25 at 13.33
  - Card 1 (2 rats in 2 quarters but has parrot): #4 at 15.42
  - Card 10 (1 rat in 1 quarter): #7 at 14.88

Conclusion: Spread rats are catastrophic. Even 2 rats in 2 quarters hurts badly
unless compensated by parrots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINDING 2: KRAKENS HAVE CAPPED PENALTY  
Status: ✅ CONFIRMED

Evidence:
  - Card 111 (2 krakens + 6 pos): Rank #6 at 15.00
  - Card 21 (2 krakens + 6 pos): Rank #6 (same score!)
  - Card 14 (2 krakens + 6 pos + parrot): Rank #2 at 15.48

Conclusion: 2+ krakens with good positives perform excellently. Kraken penalty
caps at -4 and diminishes with more krakens.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINDING 3: PARROT PREMIUM IS REAL
Status: ✅ STRONGLY CONFIRMED (with nuance)

Evidence:
  - Cards 27, 14, 24, 1 (all have parrots): Ranks #1-4
  - Card 29 with parrot + spread negatives: #5 (parrot somewhat overcame spread)
  - Card 22 with parrot + spread negatives: #26 (parrot couldn't save weak base)
  - Card 106 with NO negatives but NO parrot: Only #12

Conclusion: Parrots add ~1.0-1.5 points and can partially compensate for spread
negatives, but can't save a fundamentally weak card.

New insight: Card with ZERO negatives (106) only ranks #12, while cards WITH
negatives but WITH parrots rank #1-4. Parrots > avoiding negatives!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINDING 4: ARROWS ARE NEUTRAL → REVISED: ARROWS + PARROTS SYNERGIZE
Status: ⚠️ REVISED

Evidence:
  - Card 24 (2 arrows + parrot + clean): Rank #3 at 15.43
  - Card 27 (1 arrow + parrot + 8 pos): Rank #1 at 15.51
  - Card 9 (2 arrows + parrot): Rank #10 at 14.56
  - Card 12 (2 arrows, no parrot, rats): Rank #29 at 12.42

Conclusion: Arrows are NOT neutral — they synergize with parrots. Arrow → parrot
copies parrot value. Arrow + good base = amplification. Arrow + bad base = still bad.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINDING 5: NEGATIVE CONCENTRATION ENABLES HIDING
Status: ✅ CONFIRMED

Evidence:
  - Card 113 (3 sharks ALL in BL quarter): Rank #21 at 13.65
  - Card 25 (3 sharks in 1 quarter + weak positives): Rank #21
  - Concentrated negatives help, but still need good positives

Counter-evidence FOUND:
  - Card 29 (spread negatives in 3 quarters + parrot + 6 good positives): #5
  - Card 26 (spread negatives in 3 quarters BUT 8 positives): #9

Conclusion: Concentration helps but many strong positives can overcome spread
negatives. It's about TOTAL card power, not just negative management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINDING 6: RAW VALUE IS NOT PREDICTIVE
Status: ✅ CONFIRMED

Evidence:
  - Card 27 (RawVal +6.5): #1
  - Card 14 (RawVal +2.5): #2  
  - Card 106 (RawVal +6.0, ZERO negatives): Only #12
  - Card 107 (RawVal -2.0): #30

Conclusion: Raw value helps but distribution and parrot presence matter more.
A card with lower raw value but concentrated negatives + parrot beats a
"cleaner" card without parrots.
